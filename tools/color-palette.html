Color Harmony Explained

  • Complementary — colors opposite on the color wheel. High contrast, vibrant look.
  • Analogous — colors adjacent on the wheel. Harmonious, serene, comfortable.
  • Triadic — three colors evenly spaced (120° apart). Colorful and balanced.
  • Tetradic — four colors in two complementary pairs. Rich and varied.
  • Split-Complementary — a variation of complementary with less tension.
  • Monochromatic — different shades and tints of a single color. Clean and elegant.
