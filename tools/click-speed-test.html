About Click Speed Test

The CPS (clicks per second) test measures how fast you can click your mouse button. The average person clicks about 6-7 times per second. Pro gamers can reach 10-14 CPS.

CPS Rankings

Turtle (0-4 CPS) — Average (4-7 CPS) — Fast (7-9 CPS) — Pro (9-12 CPS) — Insane (12+ CPS)
