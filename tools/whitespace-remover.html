Why Remove Invisible Characters?

Invisible characters can cause bugs in code, break string comparisons, and hide malicious content. Common culprits include zero-width spaces (U+200B), non-breaking spaces (U+00A0), and zero-width joiners (U+200D).

What This Tool Detects

  • Zero-width spaces, joiners, and non-joiners
  • Non-breaking spaces and narrow no-break spaces
  • Soft hyphens and word joiners
  • Left-to-right and right-to-left marks
  • Extra whitespace and blank lines
