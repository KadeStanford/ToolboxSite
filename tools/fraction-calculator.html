How to Add Fractions Online

Use the fraction calculator above for any combination. For manual calculation:

  1. Adding/Subtracting: Find the LCD, convert fractions, then add/subtract numerators.
  2. Multiplying: Multiply numerators × numerators and denominators × denominators.
  3. Dividing: Multiply the first fraction by the reciprocal of the second.
  4. Simplifying: Divide numerator and denominator by their GCD.
