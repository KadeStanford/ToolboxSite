YouTube Thumbnail Best Practices

YouTube recommends thumbnails be 1280×720 pixels with a 16:9 aspect ratio. Use high-contrast text, expressive faces, and bright colors to increase click-through rates. Keep important elements away from the edges where duration overlays appear.
