What is BMI?

Body Mass Index (BMI) is a simple numerical value calculated from your weight and height. It is widely used as a screening tool to categorize individuals into weight status categories. BMI = weight(kg) / height(m)².

BMI Limitations

BMI does not directly measure body fat. Muscular athletes may have a high BMI but low body fat. Older adults may have a normal BMI but high body fat. Always consult a healthcare professional for a complete health assessment.
