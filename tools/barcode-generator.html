About Barcode Generator

Generate scannable Code128 barcodes from any text. Code128 supports all 128 ASCII characters and is widely used in logistics, shipping, and inventory management.

How to Use

Type your text, adjust bar width and height, pick colors, and download the barcode as a PNG image.
