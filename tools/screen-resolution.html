Understanding Screen Resolution

Screen resolution refers to the number of pixels displayed on your screen. Higher resolutions provide sharper images. The device pixel ratio (DPR) indicates how many physical pixels make up one CSS pixel — Retina displays typically have a DPR of 2 or more.
