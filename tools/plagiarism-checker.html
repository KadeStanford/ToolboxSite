How This Plagiarism Checker Works

This tool compares your text against pasted source material using n-gram matching. It breaks both texts into word sequences of a configurable length and finds overlapping phrases. Matching sections are highlighted in both texts.

Limitations

This is a local comparison tool — it does not search the internet. It compares only against the source material you provide. For best results, paste the specific source documents you want to compare against.
