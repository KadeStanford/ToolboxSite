Why Resize Images?

  • Web performance — Uploading oversized images slows down websites. Resize before uploading to a CMS.
  • Email attachments — Large photos can be blocked by email servers. Resize to under 1 MB.
  • Social media — Each platform has optimal dimensions. Instagram posts: 1080×1080. Twitter header: 1500×500.
  • Storage — Smaller images take less storage space on your device or cloud.
