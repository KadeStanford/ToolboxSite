How to Use the Image Color Picker

  1. Upload or drag-and-drop your image into the upload area.
  2. Hover over the image — a magnifier shows you a zoomed preview of the area under your cursor.
  3. Click any spot on the image to pick that color. The HEX, RGB, and HSL values will appear.
  4. Click any of the value labels to copy that format to your clipboard.
  5. Your last 12 picks are saved as swatches in the Color History row.

Common Uses

  • Brand color matching — Extract exact colors from a logo PNG.
  • Web design — Sample colors from a mockup to use in your CSS.
  • Photo palette — Build a color palette from a reference photograph.
