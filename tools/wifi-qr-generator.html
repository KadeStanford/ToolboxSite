WiFi QR Code Generator

Generate a QR code to share your WiFi credentials. Guests scan the code to connect instantly — no typing needed.

Advertisement

About WiFi QR Code Generator

WiFi QR codes encode your network name, password, and encryption type. When someone scans the code with their phone camera, they automatically connect to your WiFi without having to type the password manually.

How to Use

Enter your network name (SSID) and password, select the encryption type, and download the QR code. Print it and place it where guests can scan it.
