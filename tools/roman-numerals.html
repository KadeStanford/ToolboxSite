Roman Numerals Guide

Roman numerals use seven symbols: I, V, X, L, C, D, and M. Numbers are formed by combining symbols and adding values, with subtractive notation used for 4s and 9s.

Common Uses

  • Movie and TV show copyright years (e.g., MMXX = 2020)
  • Super Bowl numbering (e.g., Super Bowl LVIII = 58)
  • Clock faces and book chapters
  • Names and sequels (e.g., Henry VIII = Henry 8)
