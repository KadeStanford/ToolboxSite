How This Speed Test Works

This speed test works by downloading and uploading data to public CDN servers and measuring the throughput over time. The test runs for several seconds to allow your connection to stabilize, giving more accurate results than single-burst tests.

Understanding Your Results

Download Speed (Mbps) measures how fast data arrives at your device. Upload Speed (Mbps) measures how fast you can send data. Ping/Latency (ms) measures the round-trip time for a single request. Jitter (ms) shows how consistent your ping is — lower is better for gaming and video calls.
