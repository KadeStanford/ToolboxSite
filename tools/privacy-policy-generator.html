Why You Need a Privacy Policy

Privacy policies are legally required in most jurisdictions if you collect any personal data from visitors. They explain what data you collect, how you use it, and users' rights regarding their data.

Key Regulations

  • GDPR — Required for EU visitors. Must explain legal basis for processing, data retention, and user rights.
  • CCPA — California residents have the right to know, delete, and opt-out of sale of personal information.
  • COPPA — Special protections for children under 13 in the US.
