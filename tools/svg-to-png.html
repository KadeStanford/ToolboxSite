About SVG to PNG Converter

SVG is a vector format that scales infinitely, but sometimes you need a raster PNG. This tool renders your SVG onto a canvas at the exact resolution you specify and exports it as PNG.

How It Works

Paste SVG code or upload an SVG file. Choose a scale multiplier or set custom dimensions. Click download and get a high-quality PNG. No data leaves your browser.
