About PDF to Text

This tool uses Mozilla's PDF.js library to parse and extract text from PDF files entirely in your browser. No data is uploaded — your files stay private.

Supported PDFs

Works best with text-based PDFs. Scanned documents (images) require OCR, which this tool does not perform. Protected/encrypted PDFs may not be readable.
