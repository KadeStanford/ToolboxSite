How Much to Tip?

Tipping norms vary by country and service type. In the United States, a 15–20% tip is standard for restaurant service. Here are common guidelines:

  • Restaurants: 15–20% of pre-tax bill
  • Bars: $1–2 per drink, or 15–20% for tab
  • Taxis / Rideshare: 10–15%
  • Hotel housekeeping: $2–5 per night
  • Hair salon / spa: 15–20%
