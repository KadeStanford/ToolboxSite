About Video to GIF Converter

This tool converts video files to animated GIFs entirely in your browser using FFmpeg WebAssembly. No video data is uploaded — everything is processed locally on your device.

Features

  • Trim: Set precise start and end times to capture the perfect moment
  • Resize: Control output width — height scales proportionally
  • Frame Rate: Choose from 5–30 FPS for size vs. smoothness trade-offs
  • Quality & Colors: Adjust palette size and dithering for optimal results
  • Crop: Square, 4:3, 16:9, and portrait aspect ratios
  • Speed: Slow down to 0.25× or speed up to 4×
  • Reverse & Boomerang: Play backwards or create seamless loops
  • Text Overlay: Add captions with custom size, color, and position
