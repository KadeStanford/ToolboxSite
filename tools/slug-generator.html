About URL Slugs

A URL slug is the part of a URL that identifies a specific page in a human-readable format. For example, in the URL toolnook.net/tools/slug-generator, the slug is slug-generator.

SEO Best Practices for Slugs

  • Use hyphens (-) to separate words — Google recommends hyphens over underscores
  • Keep slugs short and descriptive — ideally 3–5 words
  • Use lowercase letters only
  • Remove stop words (a, the, is, in, etc.) to keep slugs concise
  • Include your primary keyword in the slug
  • Avoid special characters, spaces, and numbers unless necessary
