About the Text Sorter

The ToolNook Text Sorter lets you quickly sort any list of items in various ways. Whether you need to alphabetize a list of names, sort a CSV column, or shuffle items randomly, this tool handles it instantly in your browser.

Sorting Options

  • A → Z — Alphabetical ascending (case-insensitive by default)
  • Z → A — Alphabetical descending
  • Shortest/Longest first — Sort by character length
  • Numeric — Sort numbers in ascending or descending order
  • Shuffle — Randomize the order of lines
  • Reverse — Flip the current order of lines
