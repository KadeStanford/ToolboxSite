Free Browser-Based Text to Speech

This Text to Speech tool uses the Web Speech API built into modern browsers. No data is sent to any server — everything is processed locally on your device.

Features

  • Multiple voices and languages depending on your browser and OS
  • Adjustable playback speed (0.25x to 3x)
  • Pitch control for voice customization
  • Pause, resume, and stop controls
