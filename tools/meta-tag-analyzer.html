Why Check Meta Tags?

Meta tags are critical for SEO and social sharing. They tell search engines and social platforms what your page is about, how to display it, and what image to use when shared.

What We Check

  • Basic SEO — Title, description, robots, canonical URL
  • Open Graph — Facebook/LinkedIn sharing properties
  • Twitter Cards — Twitter-specific preview data
  • Technical — Charset, viewport, language, favicon
