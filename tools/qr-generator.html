How to Use QR Codes

QR codes (Quick Response codes) are two-dimensional barcodes that can be scanned by any smartphone camera. They are commonly used to share website links, contact cards, and WiFi credentials.

QR Code Types

  • URL — opens a website when scanned
  • Text — displays plain text
  • Email — opens a pre-filled email compose window
  • Phone — prompts the user to call the number
  • WiFi — connects automatically to a WiFi network
