How to Use the Age Calculator

Enter your date of birth in the first field. The "Age On Date" field defaults to today but you can change it to calculate your age on any specific date — perfect for historical or future age calculations.

What the Results Mean

  • Years / Months / Days — your precise age broken into components (e.g., 25 years, 3 months, 12 days)
  • Total Months / Total Days — your age expressed as a single total unit
  • Total Hours — approximate hours you've been alive
