What is Schema Markup?

Schema markup (structured data) is code you add to your pages to help search engines understand your content. It can lead to rich snippets in search results — star ratings, FAQ dropdowns, product prices, and more.

Supported Schema Types

  • Article — Blog posts, news articles
  • FAQ — Frequently asked questions (Google FAQ rich results)
  • Product — Products with pricing and reviews
  • Local Business — Physical business locations
  • Organization — Companies and organizations
  • Person — Individual profiles
  • Event — Events with dates and venues
  • Recipe — Cooking recipes
