How Typing Speed Is Measured

Words per minute (WPM) is calculated using the standard definition: one "word" equals 5 characters (including spaces). Your net WPM accounts for errors, while gross WPM counts all typed characters. A good typing speed is 40+ WPM, professional typists often reach 80-120 WPM.
