About the Word Counter

The ToolNook Word Counter is an instant text analysis tool that counts words, characters, sentences, paragraphs, and lines, and estimates reading time and speaking time. It updates in real time as you type or paste text.

Reading Time Estimates

  • Reading time — Based on 238 words per minute (average adult reading speed)
  • Speaking time — Based on 130 words per minute (average speaking pace)

Common Uses

This tool is helpful for writers, students, bloggers, and professionals who need to meet word count requirements for essays, articles, reports, social media posts, or SEO content guidelines.
