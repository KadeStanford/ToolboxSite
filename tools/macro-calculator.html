Macronutrient Basics

Each macro has a caloric value:

  • Protein — 4 calories per gram. Essential for muscle repair and growth.
  • Carbohydrates — 4 calories per gram. Primary energy source for high-intensity activity.
  • Fat — 9 calories per gram. Needed for hormones, vitamins absorption, and long-duration energy.

Macros for Different Goals

  • Cutting: Higher protein (35–40%) to preserve muscle. Lower carbs.
  • Bulking: Higher carbs (45–50%) for training fuel and recovery.
  • Keto: Very low carbs (5–10%), high fat (60–70%) to induce ketosis.
