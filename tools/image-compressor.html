About Image Compressor

Large images slow down websites and use bandwidth. This tool compresses images entirely in your browser using the Canvas API — your files never leave your device. Adjust the quality slider to balance size and visual quality.

How to Use

Upload an image, choose the output format and quality level, and compare the original with the compressed version. Download when satisfied with the result.
