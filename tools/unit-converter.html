About Unit Conversion

Our unit converter supports the most common conversions between metric and imperial systems. All calculations are performed client-side for instant results with no server needed.

Supported Units

  • Length: millimeter, centimeter, meter, kilometer, inch, foot, yard, mile, nautical mile
  • Weight: milligram, gram, kilogram, metric ton, ounce, pound, stone, US ton
  • Temperature: Celsius, Fahrenheit, Kelvin
  • Volume: milliliter, liter, cubic meter, US teaspoon, tablespoon, US fluid oz, US cup, US pint, US quart, US gallon, imperial gallon
