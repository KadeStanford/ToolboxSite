How to Use the Time Zone Converter

Select the date and time in your source timezone, choose the "From" timezone from the dropdown, pick the target "To" timezone, and hit Convert. The result shows the exact equivalent time in the target zone.

Popular Time Zone Conversions

  • EST to PST — US East Coast to West Coast (3 hours earlier)
  • UTC to EST — UTC to US Eastern (UTC−5 or UTC−4 in DST)
  • GMT to IST — UK to India (UTC+5:30)
  • EST to CET — New York to Central Europe (6 hours ahead)
