About Favicon Generator

A favicon is the small icon shown in browser tabs. This tool lets you quickly create one from text, an emoji, or an uploaded image, and generates it in all common sizes: 16x16, 32x32, 48x48, 64x64, 128x128, and 256x256 pixels.

How to Use

Enter text (1-4 characters) or an emoji, pick colors and shape, then download. Or switch to the Upload tab to crop an existing image into favicon sizes.
