Understanding Your Mortgage Payment

A mortgage payment consists of four components (PITI):

  • Principal — The portion that reduces what you owe.
  • Interest — The lender's charge for borrowing money.
  • Taxes — Property taxes collected and held in escrow.
  • Insurance — Homeowner's insurance (and PMI if applicable).

30-Year vs 15-Year Mortgage

A 15-year mortgage typically has a lower interest rate and you'll pay far less total interest — but monthly payments are higher. A 30-year mortgage has lower monthly payments and more flexibility, but you'll pay significantly more interest over the life of the loan.
