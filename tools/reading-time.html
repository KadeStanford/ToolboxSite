Reading Speed Reference

  • 100 WPM — Slow reader, second language, or complex technical text
  • 150 WPM — Below average
  • 200 WPM — Average adult reading speed
  • 250 WPM — Above average
  • 300 WPM — Fast reader
  • 400–600 WPM — Speed reading techniques
  • 130 WPM — Average speaking (presentations, podcasts)
  • 150 WPM — Conversational speech

For blog posts, add ~30 seconds per image or embedded element — they add visual reading time.
