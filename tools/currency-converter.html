About Currency Exchange Rates

Exchange rates fluctuate continuously based on supply and demand, economic conditions, interest rates, and geopolitical events. There are two main types:

  • Spot rate — The current market rate for immediate exchange.
  • Forward rate — A contracted rate for future exchange at a predetermined date.

Tips for Currency Exchange

  • Avoid airport currency exchanges — they often charge high fees and poor rates.
  • Credit cards with no foreign transaction fees often give you the best rates automatically.
  • Withdraw local currency from ATMs abroad for better rates than exchange bureaus.
